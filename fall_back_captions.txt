Fall back. ⏰

Clocks go back one hour at 2:00 AM this Sunday, 1 November. Enjoy the extra sleep — then do the two things that matter:

1. Test your smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
2. Replace the batteries.

Working smoke alarms on every storey and outside every sleeping area are the law in Ontario, and so are CO alarms near bedrooms if you have a fuel-burning appliance, fireplace or attached garage. Twice a year, when the clocks change, is the easiest habit to keep.

Pass this along to someone who always forgets.

Clocks fall back one hour at 2:00 AM this Sunday, 1 November.

Our twice-a-year reminder to homeowners across York Region and the GTA: test your smoke and carbon monoxide alarms and replace the batteries. Ontario's Fire Code requires working smoke alarms on every storey and outside sleeping areas, plus CO alarms near bedrooms in homes with a fuel-burning appliance, fireplace or attached garage. Alarms also have an expiry date — check the back; most are good for 10 years.

Ten minutes this weekend. Worth it.
